Ultimate Gift Tags Name Generator – Privacy Policy

Ultimate Gift Tags Name Generator does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal data.

3MF files selected by the user, names entered into the generator and generated files are processed locally within the user's browser. This information is not uploaded to or stored by the developer.

All runtime libraries and the required Life Savers ExtraBold font are packaged with the extension. The extension does not transmit the selected 3MF, entered names or generated file to external services.

No browsing history, website content, location, authentication information, financial information or other personal information is collected by the extension.
