JC Store Display Name Generator – Privacy Policy JC Store Display Name Generator does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal data. 3MF files selected by the user, names entered into the generator and generated files are processed locally within the user's browser. This information is not uploaded to or stored by the developer. The extension may retrieve open-source software libraries and font resources required for its operation from public hosting services. These requests do not include the user's selected 3MF file, entered name or generated file. No browsing history, website content, location, authentication information, financial information or other personal information is collected by the extension. Last updated: 5 October 2026
